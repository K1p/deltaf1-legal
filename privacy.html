Privacy Policy

Delta F1 publisher · last updated 31 October 2026

Delta F1 publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Delta F1 channel to upload the channel's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent (OAuth), the tool accesses only the owner's own accounts: on YouTube to upload videos and set their title, description, playlists and publish time; on TikTok to upload videos to the owner's drafts and read basic profile info; on Instagram to publish the owner's own Reels.

Data storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer (macOS Keychain). No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No data from other users is collected.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the account settings of each platform (Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).
